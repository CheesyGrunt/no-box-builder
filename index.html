How these numbers are built

Figures come directly from the Virtual Services engagement log. Volunteer time value uses the Independent Sector value of volunteer time for the year the hours were served. In-kind value is self-reported by volunteers at the point of logging.

Recipient names, volunteer names, and individual record detail are deliberately excluded. Everything shown here is aggregated.

Reading the current year

The current year is partial and will keep moving. Use the year-over-year comparison, which covers a matched window in both years, for any year-over-year claim.

A small number of logged engagements are flagged during review as potential duplicates or entry errors and are held out of supported counts.
